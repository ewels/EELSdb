The EELS Data Base is an open-access repository of spectra from electron energy-loss spectroscopy (EELS) and X-ray absorption spectroscopy (XAS) experiments.

Virginie Serin (Université Toulouse III, CEMES-CNRS) and Thierry Sikora created the first version of the database in the late 1990s.
With the help of the European microscopy network ESTEEM, they gathered a large number of spectra.
Spectroscopists, theoreticians, students and companies use it as a reference catalogue of fine structures and to test data treatment.

In 2014, Luc Lajaunie (Université de Nantes, Institut des Matériaux Jean Rouxel) took over the management of the database, and Phil Ewels rebuilt the website and the database.
[Ewels et al., Microscopy and Microanalysis 22 (2016)](https://doi.org/10.1017/S1431927616000179) describes that work.

## Share your spectra

Submit spectra from your published work, so that others can reuse your data and cite your paper when they do.
See [Submit a spectrum](/docs/share/submit/).

## Licence

The spectra are open data under the [Open Data Commons Open Database Licence (ODbL)](https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/).
You may copy, share and adapt them if you credit the EELS Data Base and its contributors, and share any adapted database under the same licence.
The rest of the website is under [CC BY-SA 2.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/2.0/).

Credit both the contributor's paper, if there is one, and the EELS Data Base. See [How to cite](/docs/about/citation/).

## Support

The IMN laboratory and the CNRS support the EELS Data Base.
The CEMES laboratory, the European microscopy network ESTEEM 2, the French microscopy network METSA and the French microscopy society SFµ funded it.

## Use the logo

- Leave clear space around the logo, at least as tall as the "E", on every side.
- Use the full logo at 240 px (5 cm in print) wide or more. Below that, use the compact logo, down to 100 px (2.5 cm) wide.
- On dark backgrounds, use the version for dark backgrounds. On photos, or when you can print only one colour, use the black or white version.
- Scale the logo only in proportion. Do not recolour, stretch or redraw the traces, and do not change or retype the lettering.
- When you show results from the database, also [cite EELSdb](/docs/about/citation/).

## Colours

Each colour has a value for light backgrounds and a value for dark backgrounds. Click a swatch to copy its value.

- Trace red: `#a21e16` on light backgrounds, `#e86f63` on dark backgrounds. The first trace in the logo and favicon. Logo only.
- Trace ochre: `#b96716` on light backgrounds, `#e3a35d` on dark backgrounds. The second trace in the logo and favicon. Logo only.
- Slate: `#345b80` on light backgrounds, `#98c3ec` on dark backgrounds. The interface accent: primary buttons, the current page, focus.
- Ink: `#1b1a19` on light backgrounds, `#ece8e2` on dark backgrounds. Text and the logo lettering.
- Graphite: `#5d5852` on light backgrounds, `#a9a29a` on dark backgrounds. Secondary text and the logo tagline.
- Paper: `#fbfaf8` on light backgrounds, `#141312` on dark backgrounds. Page background.

## Typeface

The logo and the site use Mona Sans, by GitHub.
The logo uses ExtraBold (800) for "EELS", and Regular (400) for "db" and the tagline.
Mona Sans is free to use under the SIL Open Font License.

The EELS Data Base (EELSdb) is an open collection of reference spectra from electron energy-loss spectroscopy (EELS) and X-ray absorption spectroscopy (XAS).
You can browse and download every spectrum without an account.
To share your own spectra, sign in and submit them. A moderator checks each submission before it is published.

title: Edit a spectrum
description: Change a spectrum you contributed, which changes need a moderator, and where to see the history of a spectrum.
---

You can edit the spectra you contributed. Moderators can edit any spectrum.

1. Open the spectrum page and click **Edit**.
2. Change the fields. The form is the same as the [submit form](/docs/share/submit/).
3. Click **Save changes**.

## Which changes need a moderator

Changes to the text publish at once:

- the title and the comments;
- the keywords;
- the reference;
- the associated spectra and the other links.

Changes to the data go to a moderator first, and the published spectrum stays as it is until they approve:

- a new data file;
- the type, phase, intensity units, formula, elements or edges;
- the acquisition values, such as the beam energy and angles;
- the treatment;
- the X-ray absorption fields.

One edit of this kind can wait for review at a time. If an edit is waiting, wait for the moderator's decision before you send another.

## See the history

Click **History** on the spectrum page to see every change since publication: who made it, when, and what changed.
